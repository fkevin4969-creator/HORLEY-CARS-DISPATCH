Horley Cars Customer Booking
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how Horley Cars uses personal information in the Horley Cars Customer Booking app, its customer booking website and the connected dispatch service.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Horley Cars at carlinedsm430430@gmail.com.

Request account and data deletion

Information collected

  • Contact details you provide: name and telephone number, and email address when you create an account.
  • Account information: account identifier, sign-in credentials handled by our authentication provider, and your saved profile.
  • Booking information: pickup and destination addresses and their coordinates, journey date and time, passenger count, vehicle choice, optional flight number and notes, fare estimate, booking reference and booking status.
  • Technical information needed to operate the service: IP address, request and error information, and locally stored sign-in session information.

Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in booking notes.

How information is used

We use your information to receive and manage bookings, allocate drivers, provide the journey, contact you about your booking, calculate fare estimates, show booking history, manage accounts and resolve support enquiries. Technical information is used to run the service, protect against misuse and diagnose problems.

For UK data protection purposes, booking and account information is used to take steps at your request and provide the service. Service security and support involve our legitimate interests in operating a reliable booking service. We also use or retain information where necessary to meet applicable legal obligations.

Driver location and customer location

When a driver is allocated and travelling to collect you, the app can display that driver's shared location and an estimated arrival time. The customer app's booking and tracking features use the addresses you enter and the driver's location; they do not require continuous tracking of your device. Arrival estimates depend on available location and routing data.

Who receives information

Horley Cars dispatch staff and the allocated driver receive information needed to manage and fulfil your booking, including journey and contact details.

  • Supabase provides account authentication, database and backend hosting.
  • Ideal Postcodes provides address suggestions and address resolution. Address search text and selected address identifiers are sent to this service through our backend.
  • OpenStreetMap/Nominatim provides airport address searches and map tiles. Map services receive map requests and technical connection information.
  • The public OSRM routing service receives journey coordinates to calculate road distance, routes and arrival estimates.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the booking website and public information pages. jsDelivr and unpkg deliver software resources used by the app. These services receive technical connection information when resources are requested.

We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to handle a relevant legal claim. The current customer app does not include advertising or advertising analytics.

Storage, security and international processing

Information is stored in the connected account and booking database. Sign-in session information is stored on your device so you can remain signed in. Signing out clears the active local session; uninstalling the app does not delete server-side account or booking records.

The app uses HTTPS connections for its backend and external services. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Providers may process technical or service information outside the UK; their handling of that information is governed by their applicable terms and privacy notices.

How long information is kept

Account details remain in the service while your account is retained. Booking records remain available for booking history and operational records until they are removed or anonymised. The current app does not automatically erase booking history after a fixed period.

When deciding whether information must be retained after a deletion request, we consider outstanding journeys or disputes and applicable licensing, accounting, security and legal record-keeping obligations. We will explain any relevant exception and retention period when responding to your request. Information no longer needed should be deleted or anonymised.

Request account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your Horley Cars Customer Booking account and associated personal information without reinstalling the app.

  1. Email carlinedsm430430@gmail.com with the subject Horley Cars Customer Booking account deletion.
  2. Send the request from your account email address where possible. Include your account email and say whether you want the account and associated data deleted, or only specified booking data.
  3. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you. Do not send your password.

Deletion requests are handled by Horley Cars staff. They cover your sign-in account, saved profile and associated personal booking information, subject to any necessary legal or record-keeping exception explained above. Deleting an account is different from cancelling a taxi booking; contact Horley Cars separately about any active journey.

You can also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.
